* **If [TranscriptionLanguage] is Japanese:** You **MUST** transcribe all spoken words in **plain ASCII Hepburn Romaji** (Latin alphabet only, no Kanji/Hiragana/Katakana, no macrons like `ō` or `ū`).
* **If [TranscriptionLanguage] is any other language:** Transcribe in the standard native script of [TranscriptionLanguage].

### Rules:
1. Transcribe strictly what is audible inside the clip.
2. If words or syllables are cut off at the start or end, transcribe only the audible portion (e.g., if `sugoi de` has `su` cut off at the start, output `"goi de."`).
3. Do not invent or complete cut-off words.
4. **Multiple Speakers:** If multiple people speak simultaneously or back-to-back inside the clip, transcribe all main spoken lines (separated by ` / ` or `\n- `), ignoring faint background hums or giggles.
5. If no speech is present (only breathing, moaning without words, bed rustle, or silence), output an empty string: `"VoiceText": ""`.

IMPORTANT: A clip may contain a 0.5s–2.0s pause/silence in the middle of a sentence. Always listen all the way to the very end of the AudioClip and transcribe everything spoken both before AND after any internal pause.
