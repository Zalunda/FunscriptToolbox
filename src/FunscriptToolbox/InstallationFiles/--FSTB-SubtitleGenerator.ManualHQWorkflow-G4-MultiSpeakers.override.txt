# MULTI-SPEAKER & OVERLAPPING DIALOGUE ADDENDUM

### Multi-Speaker Segmentation & Anti-Bundling Mandate
When multiple speakers talk in a scene, keep timestamps strictly sequential (`EndTime` of segment $N \le$ `StartTime` of segment $N+1$) and follow these strict rules to prevent lazy bundling:

1. **Sequential Turn-Taking Where Each Speaker's Line is >= 1.2s (MUST BE SPLIT into Separate Segments!):**
   * Whenever Speaker A speaks a sentence (`>= 1.2s`) and Speaker B follows with her own sentence (`>= 1.2s`), you **MUST** output them as **separate sequential segments**—even if there is zero silence between them or their voices slightly overlap for `100–300ms` at the handoff!
   * **Examples of Lazy Bundling (STRICTLY FORBIDDEN):**
     * *Bad (4.5s bundled):* `- De-mo, wa-ta-shi-ta-chi wa ma-ke-ma-se-n yo.\n- Ne. Ze-tta-i ka-chi-ma-su.` $\rightarrow$ **SPLIT** into Segment 1 (`1.8s`: `De-mo, wa-ta-shi-ta-chi wa ma-ke-ma-se-n yo.`) and Segment 2 (`2.0s`: `Ne. Ze-tta-i ka-chi-ma-su.`)!
     * *Bad (4.3s bundled):* `- Ho-n-to-u ni o-tsu-yo-i de-su ne.\n- Ko-n-ka-i mo i-ppa-tsu de-su.` $\rightarrow$ **SPLIT** into Segment 1 (`1.9s`: `Ho-n-to-u ni o-tsu-yo-i de-su ne.`) and Segment 2 (`2.0s`: `Ko-n-ka-i mo i-ppa-tsu de-su.`)!
     * *Bad (8.5s bundled group reaction + solo lines):* Bundling `Ha-i, ha-i, ha-i` + `E, su-go-i! Tsu-yo-i!` + `Ze-tta-i ka-te-ru to o-mo-tta no-ni.` + `Ya-ri-ma-shi-ta ne.` into one 8.5s block $\rightarrow$ **SPLIT** into 4 separate segments (`1.5s`, `2.5s`, `1.9s`, `1.8s`)!
     * *Bad (12.3s announcement + group cheer):* Bundling an announcement with other girls cheering `I-ki-ma-sho-u! / I-e-e-i!` $\rightarrow$ **SPLIT** the announcement into single-speaker segments (`<= 4.5s`), and put the girls' `I-ki-ma-sho-u! / I-e-e-i!` in its own `2.5s` segment at the end!

2. **Directional Boundary Bleed Attributes (`"Overlap": "BleedLeft" | "BleedRight" | "BleedBoth"`):**
   * When you slice sequential segments at a handoff point where two speakers' voices touch or overlap, add the `"Overlap"` attribute to indicate **which boundary** of the segment contains voice bleed from an adjacent or background speaker:

     * **`"Overlap": "BleedRight"`:** The next speaker's voice (or a background reaction) bleeds into the **end** of this segment before the current speaker finishes.
     * **`"Overlap": "BleedBoth"`:** Both the start (left edge) and end (right edge) of this segment have voice bleed from adjacent/background speakers (or voices overlap throughout a simultaneous `"Speaker": "Multiple"` segment).
     * *(If a segment has clean boundaries with no other speaker bleeding in, omit the `"Overlap"` attribute.)*

3. **When to Use `"Speaker": "Multiple"` (strictly `<= 3.5s` — Simultaneous Outbursts or `< 1.0s` Micro-Turns ONLY):**
   * Only group two (or three) speakers into a single `"Speaker": "Multiple"` segment (formatted with `- Line 1\n- Line 2`) when:
     1. **True Simultaneous Outburst (`1.5s – 3.0s`):** Multiple women exclaim **at the exact same time** on top of each other (e.g., all gasping `- E? E-e! Su-go-i!\n- Tsu-yo-i!` over the same `2.5s` window when cards are revealed—and then immediately cut a **new** segment when one girl speaks a full sentence like `Ko-n-na no ha-ji-me-te mi-ma-shi-ta yo!` or `Ze-tta-i ka-te-ru to o-mo-tta no-ni.`!).
     2. **Sub-Second Rapid Turn-Taking (`< 1.0s`):** Two ultra-short interjections occur back-to-back where slicing them apart would create an unreadable `< 1.0s` micro-subtitle (e.g., `- Cho-tto, do-i-te yo.\n- Yo-i-sho.` in `2.5s`, or `- I-ki-ma-sho-u!\n- I-e-e-i!` in `2.2s`).
